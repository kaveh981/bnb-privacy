Kaveh Stays · bnb-local

Privacy and data deletion

Kaveh Stays and bnb-local are operated by 10776840 Canada Inc.

Effective 13 October 2026

bnb-local is a private guest-communication assistant used by the host of Kaveh Stays to manage accommodation enquiries and prepare replies. This notice describes the information processed by the assistant.

Information processed

The assistant may process guest names, contact and messaging identifiers, messages, reservation details, stay dates, communication preferences, and property information. It stores message source references and relevant original notification or webhook content so the host can understand where information came from.

Sources may include information entered by the host, property calendars, Airbnb-related notification emails from a connected Gmail account, and messages delivered to a connected WhatsApp Business Platform number. Gmail access is read-only; the app filters imports to Airbnb-related messages. Connecting a source does not import an entire WhatsApp chat history.

How information is used

Information is used to understand guest requests, check availability, prepare reply suggestions, and let the host review and correct them. Suggested replies and final corrections may be retained for comparison and evaluation. The current application does not send replies automatically or automatically train a model on corrections.

Storage and service providers

The host stores imported information and reply records in a local database. Credentials are stored separately in local configuration and credential files. These files are excluded from the project's source repository. Local storage is not encrypted by the application; protection also depends on the host's device and backup security.

Google, Airbnb, and Meta process information through their respective services. WhatsApp webhook traffic may pass through Cloudflare to reach the host's local receiver. The public policy page does not provide access to the assistant or its guest records.

If the host enables OpenAI draft generation, selected guest messages and relevant conversation, booking, property, and communication-style context are sent to OpenAI to generate a draft. Demo mode does not make these AI requests. The app requests that generated responses not be stored through the API's response-storage option; provider processing and retention remain subject to that provider's policies and the host's account settings.

Retention

The current application has no automatic retention deadline. Imported messages, drafts, corrections, and evaluation records remain on the host's device until the host deletes them. Disconnecting an integration stops future access but does not itself remove previously imported records. Copies may also exist in host-managed backups or the original messaging services.

Contact and deletion requests

To ask about your information or request access, correction, or deletion from bnb-local, contact the Kaveh Stays host through your existing booking conversation or the WhatsApp conversation you use with the host. Mention “bnb-local data request” and provide enough information to identify the relevant booking or conversation. Do not send passwords or identity documents.

The host may ask for proportionate verification before acting on a request. Deletion is handled manually and covers applicable local message records, related drafts and evaluation records, and host-controlled backup copies where practicable. Any information that must be retained for booking administration or applicable obligations, and any limits on deletion, will be explained in the response. Deleting information from bnb-local does not automatically delete the original messages held by Airbnb, Google, or Meta.

Changes

This notice should be updated before enabling materially different sources, processing, or sharing. The published version will show its effective date.
